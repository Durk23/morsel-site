What Morsel is

A free, swipe-through Bible for iPhone and iPad. Full-screen verse cards you flick through, a heart to keep the ones that hit, and the whole Bible one tap away from any card — with reading plans, highlights, notes, search, read-aloud, and a verse every morning.

Morsel is free for everyone, forever. No ads, no accounts, no subscriptions, and nothing is ever locked. Every feature works offline.

Your privacy

Nothing leaves your phone. Morsel has no server, no analytics, and no tracking of any kind — its App Store privacy label is “Data Not Collected,” because nothing is. Your saved verses, highlights, notes, and prayer list sync through your own iCloud, which we never see.

Common questions

Which Bible is this? The World English Bible (WEB), a public-domain translation in modern English. By default God's name is printed “the LORD,” as in most English Bibles; Settings can show “Yahweh” as the WEB prints it. That display choice is the only change Morsel ever makes to scripture.

Where did my highlights go after a new phone? They ride your iCloud account. Make sure you're signed into the same Apple Account, and give it a minute. You can also carry a backup file: Settings › Back up.

What do the tips in Settings unlock? Nothing — on purpose. Gifts go toward licensing more translations and recording a human narration, free for everyone, like everything else.

Why no account or sign-in? Because none is needed. Reading the Bible shouldn't require handing anyone your data.

Scripture quotations are from the World English Bible, public domain.
